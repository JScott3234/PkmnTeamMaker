TeamMaker - Competitive Pokemon Team Builder

Used for choosing pokemon of varying competitive classes to build teams based on preferred strategies.

User should be able to choose what pokemon they want, and the program gives suggestions to ensure the team is still viable to use!

Ideally, a score could be given to determine how well a team is built, in measured by defensive type coverage, offensive capabilites, useful abilities, and synergy of strategies for a win-con.

Pokemon are categorized by their competitive roles, such as:
- Sweepers (Physical, Special, Mixed)
- Walls (Physical, Special, Mixed)
- Support (Hazards, Screens, Clerics, etc.)
- Utility (Lead, Pivot, etc.)

Pokemon are also categorized by their competitive tiers, such as: (Is there a way to track Smogon Tiers?)
- OU (Overused)
- UU (Underused)
- RU (Rarely Used)
- NU (Never Used)
- PU (Partially Used)
- Uber (Banned from OU)
- AG (Anything Goes)

Pokemon are also categorized by their competitive strategies, such as:
- Hyper Offense
- Balance
- Stall
- Bulky Offense
- Weather
- Trick Room
- Baton Pass
- Hazard Stacking
- Anti-Meta

Pokemon are also categorized by their competitive viability, such as:
- Top Tier
- High Tier
- Mid Tier
- Low Tier
- Bottom Tier
Which are all determined by IV and EV spreads, as well as movepools, abilities, and items.
